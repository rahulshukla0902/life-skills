# Learning Process

## How to Learn Faster with the Feynman Technique

**1. What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.**

"If you can't explain it easily, you don't understand it well enough." To gain the entire knowledge of a subject we must be able to teach someone who does not have the same base knowledge as us.


## Learning How to Learn TED talk by Barbara Oakley

**2. In this video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?**

The most interesting idea for me is the explanation of focussed and diffused mode of brain. Infact, I have personally experienced this diffused mode where new ideas for solving a problem come up in the mind when I'm doing something unrelated to the problem.


**3. What are active and diffused modes of thinking?**

Active mode of thinking refers to the state of our mind when we are focussed and actively thinking towards learning or solving a problem.

Diffused mode of thinking referes to the state of our mind when we are giving our brain a little time to relax therefore creating space for new ideas to come in.



## Learn Anything in 20 hours

**4. According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic? Only mention the points.**

There are four steps to learn a new topic:

1. Deconstruct the topic
2. Learn enough to self correct
3. Remove practice barriers or distractions
4. Practice atleast 20 hours



## Learning Principles in the Bootcamp

**5. What are some of the actions you can take going forward to improve your learning process?**

Some points that I will keep in mind throughout the bootcamp and afterwards are as follows:
* Understand each concept in a way such that I can teach it in easy words.
* Practice every concept enough to remove the initial emotional barrier of not feeling confident about it.
* Deconstuct every topic to identify every goal and then proceed strategically.
* Divide time for study and relax separately and study when studying, relax when relaxing.
* Try to use diffused mode of thinking more often to let the mind grow out of a certain loop of thoughts.
