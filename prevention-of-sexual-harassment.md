# Prevention of Sexual Harassment
---

**1. What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?**

Sexual harassment can be caused by variour scenarios. Some of them are listed below:
* Showcasing or saying suggestive and objectionable things.
* Staring, long handshake, commenting indirectly about someone.
* Inappropriately touching, hugging, and rubbing.
* Transmission of offensive messages, emails, texts or images.
* Asking unwarranted and repeated questions.
* Enquiring about personal/sexual life.



**2. What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?**

If I face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of objectionable behaviour, I will follow the below steps:
* Politely ask for stopping of such behaviour.
* Document the incidents and keep proofs of the sequence of events.
* Report to the Internal Complaints Committee(ICC) about the incident with adequate proofs.
