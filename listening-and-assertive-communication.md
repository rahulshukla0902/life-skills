# Listening and Assertive Communication

## Active Listening

**1. What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?**

* Focus on the speaker and the topic.
* Avoid getting distracted by our own thoughts.
* Try not to interrupt the speaker and let them finish before responding.
* Use door opening phrases to show your interest and keep the other person talking.
* Take notes in important conversations.
* Paraphrase to make sure you're understanding correctly.



## Reflective Listening

**2. According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?**
* Without interrupting, listen carefully
* Repeat what your heard
* Ask questions to clarify any communication gap
* Confirm your undersanding


## Reflection

**3. What are the obstacles in your listening process?**
Some of the obstacles in our listening process:
* Distraction due to our own thoughts.
* Lack of focus and the urge to reply rather than respond.
* Non-usage of door opening phrases therefore not encouraging the speakers to speak their thoughts.
* Not paraphrasing, therefore giving scope for misunderstandings.



**4. What can you do to improve your listening?**
* Completely focus on the speaker and the topic and avoid distractions.
* Paraphrasing constantly to increase clarity of thought and showcase interest.
* Never interrupt the speaker and always listen before speaking.
* Try to respond after thinking instead of just replying.
