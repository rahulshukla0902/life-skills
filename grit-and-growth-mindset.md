## Grit

**1. Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines. Use your own words.**

Grit is sustained passion and long-term perseverance that results in extraordinary achievements more often than talent or IQ.

## Introduction to Growth Mindset

**2. Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines in your own words.**

A "growth mindset" simply means believing that you aren't stuck with the skills you were born with—you can get smarter and better at things if you put in the work.

## Understanding Internal Locus of Control

**3. What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?**


Internal Locus of Control means believing that our actions, decisions and efforts influence our results. The key point is to focus on what we can control, take responsibility for our actions and recognise how our efforts help us improve.


## How to build a Growth Mindset

**4. What are the key points mentioned by speaker to build growth mindset (explanation not needed).**

* Believe in your ability to figure things out.
* Question your assumptions.
* Create your own plan for growth.
* Honour the struggle/hardwork.
